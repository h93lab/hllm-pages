FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is an API key and where do I get one?

An API key lets apps use your AI provider account. You create one in your provider's developer console — for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google AI Studio or OpenRouter — and paste it into HLLM once. Some providers need billing enabled first.

How much will it cost me?

HLLM is free. Your provider charges for what you use, usually fractions of a cent per message. HLLM shows token counts and an estimated cost on every reply so there are no surprises.

Can HLLM read my chats or my key?

No. There is no HLLM server between you and your provider. Your key is kept in your device's secure storage and your chats are stored only on your device.

Which models can I use?

Any chat model your provider gives you access to. HLLM tests your key and only turns on features — like images, documents or web search — that your model supports.

Does the $1.99 purchase carry over between iPhone and Android?

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, so they restore on devices that use the same store account. They don't transfer between iPhone and Android.

How do I report a problem or a harmful AI reply?

Tap ⋯ under any reply and choose Report, or email support@h93lab.com. See the support page for more.
