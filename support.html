Frequently asked questions

Where do I get an API key?

Create an account and an API key with your AI provider (for example OpenAI, Anthropic or Google), then paste the key into HLLM's provider setup. HLLM never issues keys and never stores them outside your device's secure storage.

Why does the connection test fail?

Check the base URL, the key and the selected model, and confirm your provider account has access and credit. HLLM reports the provider's own error (for example 401, 403, 404, 429 or a network error) so you can act on it. A failed test does not mean a capability is available or unavailable for sure — re-run diagnostics after fixing the cause.

Who pays for the AI usage?

You do. HLLM is a bring-your-own-key client, so your provider bills you directly for the requests you make from your own key. HLLM shows estimated cost only when a price is known, and always labels provider-reported usage separately from estimates.

How do I restore the "Remove ads" purchase?

Open Settings and tap "Restore purchases" while signed in to the same store account that made the purchase. The entitlement does not transfer between iOS and Android. If restore does not find it, confirm you are using the same Apple ID or Google account and contact us with your store receipt.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual conversations from the library, or use Settings to delete all local data (chats, attachments and provider secrets). HLLM has no account or server database, so there is no server copy to delete.

Why do I see ads?

The free version shows one banner on the library screen after you have used a chat. The one-time "Remove ads" purchase removes it. See the Privacy Policy for how ads handle data.

Is my chat private?

Your messages go directly from your device to the provider you chose, never through HLLM. See the Transparency page for the exact list of destinations.
