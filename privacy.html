HLLM Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-25

Privacy Terms Transparency Support

HLLM is a "bring your own key" (BYOK) client. You connect the app to your own AI provider account. This policy explains what stays on your device, what leaves it, and what HLLM itself receives. Contact: support@h93lab.com.

1. Your AI messages go straight to your provider

HLLM has no server in the AI path. When you send a message, the request goes directly from your device to the provider you chose (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint). HLLM cannot read those requests and does not relay, copy or store them on any HLLM infrastructure. Your provider's own privacy policy and terms apply to that data.

2. API keys stay in device secure storage

Provider API keys and search keys are stored only in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore via secure storage). They are never written to the app database, logs, exports, reports or backups, and they are never sent to HLLM.

3. Data kept on your device

  • Chats, titles, attachments and usage/cost records are stored locally in the app database.
  • Request Inspector artifacts are stored locally with keys and secret headers redacted.
  • No HLLM account exists, so there is nothing to sync to a server.

Dictation and text-to-speech use your operating system's speech services (Apple or Google); audio may be processed by those services under their privacy policies. HLLM adds no speech server of its own, and transcripts stay editable on your device until you send them. HLLM also runs no developer analytics on your usage: there is no usage-tracking SDK in the app.

4. What the report form sends

If you report an AI message, the app sends only these fields to report.h93lab.com: the selected category, the app version, your operating system, and — only if you explicitly turn it on — the message text, plus any note you type. Message text is off by default. Reports never include your keys, provider URLs, model ids or other chat history. If the request fails, the app opens a prefilled email to support@h93lab.com with the same fields. Reports are kept for 90 days and then deleted automatically. Your IP address is never stored; it is only used, as a salted and daily-rotated hash, to limit how many reports can be sent per hour.

5. Optional crash reports (off by default)

Crash reporting is disabled by default and is not initialized until you turn it on in Settings. When enabled, crash diagnostics are sent to Sentry. The app is configured to send no message text, no keys or headers, no URLs with query strings, no breadcrumbs and no session replay. You can turn it off at any time, and a preview of the diagnostics data is available in the app before sharing.

6. Ads

The free version shows a single banner at the bottom of the side menu. Ads are served by Google AdMob with consent handled by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where required. AdMob may receive device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), an approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads. Ads are requested non-personalized where consent for personalization is not granted. AdMob never receives your chat content, API keys or message text. Buying "Remove ads" stops ad requests.

7. Purchases

The one-time "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play. HLLM does not receive or store your payment details. The stores process the transaction under their own privacy policies.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete individual conversations or all local data from Settings. Deleting all data removes chats, attachments and provider secrets from your device. Because HLLM has no account or server database, there is no server-side copy to delete.

9. Children

HLLM is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change. Questions or requests: support@h93lab.com.
