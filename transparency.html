HLLM Transparency

Effective date: 2026-09-25

Privacy Terms Transparency Support

This page lists every destination HLLM itself contacts, and confirms that AI requests never pass through HLLM infrastructure.

1. AI requests never touch HLLM

When you send a chat, the request goes directly from your device to the AI provider you configured, authenticated with your own key. HLLM operates no server in that path, so it cannot see your prompts, attachments, responses or usage. The same is true for optional web-search requests made with your own search key. 

[your device] --HTTPS + your key--> [your AI provider]        (never through HLLM)
[your device] --HTTPS + your key--> [your search API]         (optional)
[your device] --HTTPS, no keys---> [Cloudflare: config/legal/report]
[your device] --HTTPS------------> [AdMob/UMP, Apple/Google stores, Sentry]

2. Exact destinations the app itself contacts

Host Why When
report.h93lab.com Report intake Worker (category, app version, OS, optional message text/note) Only when you submit the in-app report form
h93lab.com Static remote config (config.json, presets.json) and these legal pages On config refresh or when you open a legal page
Google AdMob / UMP Ad serving and consent management (device identifiers, ad data; non-personalized where consent is not granted) Only while ads are enabled, on the eligible library screen
Apple App Store / Google Play Processing the one-time "Remove ads" purchase and restoring entitlement Only when you buy or restore
Sentry Crash diagnostics, scrubbed of text, keys, headers, URL queries and breadcrumbs Only after you opt in (off by default)

Your chosen AI provider and optional search provider are third parties you select, not HLLM destinations. Their hosts appear in your own provider settings.

3. How keys are stored and protected

  • API keys and search keys live only in device secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).
  • Keys are excluded from the app database, logs, exports and Android/iOS backups.
  • Credentialed requests use HTTPS with redirects refused, so a key is never forwarded to another origin.
  • Network & Privacy in the app is an HLLM request log, not an exhaustive device monitor: it lists hosts HLLM contacts with the hostname only, never paths or query strings.

4. How to verify this yourself

Run the app behind a proxy that records TLS destinations (for example a local mitmproxy or your router's DNS log with a development build). Send a chat and confirm the connection terminates at your provider's host, not at any h93lab.com host. Then use the report form and config refresh; only those actions should reach h93lab.com / report.h93lab.com.
